YT-Content-Engine

A private production tool for one YouTube channel.

YT-Content-Engine turns long gameplay recordings made by the channel operator into short vertical clips for YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Accepts gameplay recordings uploaded by the operator and keeps an archive copy in the operator's own Google Drive.
  • Uses video-understanding services to find candidate highlight moments in those recordings.
  • Shows the candidates to the operator, who approves or rejects each one.
  • Renders approved clips and uploads them to the operator's YouTube channel.
  • Reads that channel's YouTube Analytics to learn which clips perform well.

Who uses it

The application is used only by its operator to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

Google data

The application connects to the operator's Google account to upload videos to YouTube, read channel analytics, and store archive files in Google Drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.
